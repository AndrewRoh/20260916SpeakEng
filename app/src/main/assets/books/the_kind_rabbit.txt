Once upon a time, a little rabbit lived in the forest. Her name was Rosy. One day, Rosy saw a turtle stuck under a big rock. The turtle could not move. Rosy did not walk away. She pushed the rock with all her strength. Finally, the rock rolled to the side. The turtle was free! The turtle said, "Thank you, Rosy. You are very kind." From that day, the rabbit and the turtle became best friends. They played together every single day.
